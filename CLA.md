## How to agree

Before your first pull request can be merged, you agree to this agreement
by:

- ticking the CLA box in the pull request template, and
- adding this line, with your full name, to the pull request description or
  a comment on it:

  > I have read the Agentnagar Individual Contributor Licence Agreement
  > (CLA.md, version 1.0) and I agree to it for my Contributions.
  > Name: \<your full name\>

Your agreement covers all your Contributions to the Project, past and future,
until you tell SJL in writing that it no longer covers future ones.

**If you contribute on behalf of a company or other organisation**, it needs a
corporate agreement with SJL before your Contributions can be accepted. Open
an issue saying so, and SJL will arrange it (see
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
