R1 · Starter home

A pending cash order holds the JC route so the kit cannot be bought twice. You can cancel it, and it expires by itself after 60 seconds or at the next civic period. It creates no order, charge or subscription. A real integration would verify capture through the city backend and expire on the provider's timeout.

Credits from money and tiers (RD07)

Open question (RD07/RD08). Credits are meant to pay for real compute and storage as well as game items. Which balance may pay for them—earned, tier-included or purchased—and with what caps, refunds and expiry, is not decided. This lab therefore offers no compute purchase.

    The 4,200 JC worked example plus a labelled external account of 1,800 JC. “External · payments fixture” stands in for issuance against verified real-money receipts; it is outside the city, finite here, and the only place tier and purchased credits come from. No action changes the total.

    What this leaves out

    No accounts, server, payment SDK, cash valuation, real resident data, durable save, taxes in real currency, or multiplayer. Prices, the threshold and the rate are invented. The payments fixture moves fictional credits only; no money, order, refund or subscription exists. Credit expiry, refunds of purchased credits, transfers between residents and no-cash-out rules are open. No loans or player markets are implemented.
