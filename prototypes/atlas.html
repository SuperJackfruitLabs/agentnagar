First slice · RD01 · fictional illustration

Walk the city. Watch the Guild at work.

The first thing to build is a person moving around the city and seeing the fourteen Guild agents doing their actual current work. This board sketches it: each agent is a marker in the district of its main workplace, and each marker opens a work-state card. Every name is from the Guild brief; every task, state and timestamp is invented. No adapter, runtime or network is connected.

Visitors, residents and sponsors · decisions of 18 September 2026

Three routes to a home. One way to watch.

Public visitors walk the city and watch the Guild at work; they cannot interact with agents (RD03). Residency is a saved home in the Orchard Neighbourhoods, and it is separate from sponsoring the lab (RD10). Select a proposed lifestyle below to preview it.

Buy a block

A one-time payment buys a block for a house and makes the buyer a resident. What the price includes, and for how long the home is hosted, is open.

RD09 · decided
Sponsor the lab

Sponsorship funds the lab and its product development. It may come with residency and other perks, but it is a different thing from buying a home.

RD10 · decided
Earn it by contribution

Reviewed, accepted contributions could qualify someone for residency without paying. Review criteria and thresholds are not set.

Still a proposal
Just visit

Observe the city and the Guild's public work state. A free registered account is where interaction starts, widening with tier and granted authority.

RD03 · RD04

City credits (JC — working label, rename pending, RD06) can be earned in the game, bought with real money, or included with a resident tier; a tier can be a subscription or a one-time purchase (RD07). Credits buy real things such as facility access, items, compute and storage (RD08); pricing, earned-versus-purchased balances and no-cash-out rules are open. The economy lab shows the balances.
