## What you may do

- Refer to Agentnagar and SJL by name to say truthfully what your work is,
  for example "a fork of Agentnagar", "based on Agentnagar" or "compatible
  with Agentnagar", as long as nobody could think your work is official or
  endorsed by SJL.
- Use the names in articles, reviews, teaching material and discussion.
- Build and run the unmodified source for yourself.

## What forks and redistributors must do

If you distribute a modified version, or a build you made yourself:

- give it a different name that is not confusingly similar to Agentnagar;
- replace the app icon;
- change the application and bundle id from `org.superjackfruit.*` to one of
  your own;
- replace "Super Jackfruit Labs (OPC) Private Limited" as company name and
  copyright holder of the build in `city/godot/export_presets.cfg` (keep the copyright notices that
  credit SJL and the contributors for the code and assets you use);
- do not suggest that SJL made, endorses or supports your version.

Your obligations under the AGPL and CC BY-SA, including keeping copyright
notices and offering source code, still apply.

company names) belong to their owners and are used only to refer to them.
