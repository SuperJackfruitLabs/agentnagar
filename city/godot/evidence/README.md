# Client evidence

Captures of the running Godot client, made by the tools in `godot/tools/`
and described in the `*-notes.md` files beside them. They are records of
what the client drew at the time, not marketing images.

## Composites with concept art

The comparison composites `*-vs-sheet.png` and `*-vs-sheets.png` set the
client's captures beside crops of the selected style-study concept sheets of
styles 02, 06, 08, 09, 10 and 11 in
[docs/vision/style-studies](../../../docs/vision/style-studies/README.md)
(`tools/sheet_compare.py` picks them). Those sheets are **AI-generated**:
each carries OpenAI's C2PA content credentials (gpt-image). They are
released under CC0 1.0 with no rights claimed; see
[AI-generated images](../../../docs/vision/style-studies/README.md#ai-generated-images).
