# Low-poly tropical against sheet 11

`lowpoly-vs-sheet.png` puts the running client (tick 120, 11:48, and tick
330, 20:12, public view, no HUD) beside sheet 00's Top-down, Diagonal and
Street panels and sheet 02's Night and rain panel. The individual captures
are `lowpoly-{topdown,diagonal,street,night,fpv}.png`.

## What now matches

- The composition: river and arched bridge to the west, the guild hall
  west of the square, the banyan in the middle, the library with its
  copper roof, dome and yellow banners to the east, the tram and its stop
  to the south, towers to the north, a grid of streets and terracotta
  houses around.
- Whole buildings: the hall's brick-and-timber bays and glazed sawtooth
  roofs, the library's arched two-storey bays, portico and ribbed dome.
- The square: patterned sandstone paving, a ring of benches round the
  banyan, lamp posts with banners, planters, flower beds, bollards.
- People and agents: chunky faceted people with varied outfits, hair,
  hats and backpacks; white-and-yellow robots with black face plates and
  cyan eyes; they walk, sit, type and idle.
- Light: a warm midday under a blue sky with low-poly clouds; a blue night
  with lit windows, lamps and lanterns.

## What still differs, and why it stays

- **Tree density.** The sheets pack palms into every gap. Open lawn is now
  planted, but lots and verges inside the core stay open where the
  district's rooms and walks are; filling them would put trees on
  walkable ground.
- **The hall's roof.** The sheet draws three gables facing the square; the
  manifest says sawtooth, so the hall has glazed sawtooth teeth running
  north–south (the same reading in every style).
- **Scale of the far field.** The sheet's town runs to the horizon with
  hills behind; ours ends at the fixture's scenery, with lawn beyond.
- **Night and rain.** Rain, wet reflections and umbrellas carried by
  people are not modelled; the night is dry.
- **Faces and clothes.** Faces are simple eye blocks; tees are one colour,
  where the sheet's are two-tone with white sleeves.
- **Crowd size.** The fixture crowd is 60 people; the sheet's square is
  busier.
