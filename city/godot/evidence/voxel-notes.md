# Voxel against sheet 02

`voxel-vs-sheet.png` puts the running client (tick 120, 11:48, public view,
no HUD) beside sheet 00's Top-down, Diagonal and Street panels; the other
captures are `voxel-{night,fpv,open}.png`.

## What now matches

- The yellow workshop under stepped sawtooth roofs with glazing facing the
  square, and its WORKSHOP board; the orange barrel-vault library with
  white rounded ends, blue windows and its LIBRARY board.
- Grey paved square with a big voxel tree, benches round it, lamps,
  bollards, planters and flower beds; the white-and-orange tram and its
  shelters; a blue river with stone quays and a grey bridge.
- White towers with blue glass and rooftop greens, white and cream houses
  with blue and orange roofs on a street grid with kerbs and dashed lines.
- Blocky people (skinned to the shared rig, walking, sitting, typing) and
  the pilot's robots at their desks.
- Night: deep blue with every window and lamp lit.

## What still differs, and why it stays

- **Sheet density.** The sheets pave almost every lot and fill it with
  trees and people; our lots follow the fixture's scenery and the crowd is
  60 people.
- **Smooth versus stepped curves.** The sheet's vault and sawtooth slopes
  are smooth; ours step at 10 cm, which is the voxel style's own grain.
- **The river's edge.** The sheet's quays curve; the fixture's river is a
  rectangle.

The concept-sheet panels in `voxel-vs-sheet.png` are crops of AI-generated concept art
(CC0 1.0); see [the evidence README](README.md).
