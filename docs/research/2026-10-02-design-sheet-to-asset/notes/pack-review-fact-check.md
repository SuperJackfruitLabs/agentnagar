# Fact-check of the pack's descriptions against the concept sheets (second agent, 2026-10-02 about 04:40)

Saved from the reviewer's report. It looked at the 24 selected sheets and checked 103 per-style bullets in SUBJECTS.md and the style tables in STYLES.md. Fixes belong in `asset-studies/data/styles.json` and `subjects.json`. Nine bullets could not be judged (things absent from the sheets, or too small).

## Low-poly tropical
Contradicted:
- Towers: "a small roof pavilion under a green copper cap". Flat roofs with small gold finials or roof plants in every panel; green copper is only on the library.
- Bollard: "one pale band near the top". Plain dark posts with a rounded or chamfered cap, no band.
- Guild hall: "a dark timber and steel frame". The timber is bright orange-brown; only the glazing bars are dark. (STYLES has it right.)
- Sailboat: "a yellow and red hull". Hulls are red or brown; the yellow is a sail panel.
Not supported:
- Houses: "pastel walls", "shuttered windows". All cream, with plain dark-teal panes.
Missing:
- The café's only close view (night, build mode) is a cream stone arcade with round arches, pink-flowering climbers and black iron wall lanterns.
- The great tree is called "a banyan" but no aerial roots are drawn.
- A1 has a black neck and black joints.
Palette: right family throughout.

## Voxel
Contradicted:
- Bollard: "A grey stone cube post". Dark charcoal (#434754).
- Houses: "stepped roofs". Flat parapet roofs with roof gardens and blue roof panels.
- STYLES "roof library: orange vault cubes with white ribs". Seams are orange; white is only the two rounded end caps.
- STYLES "no slopes" and "stepped cube roofs". The sheets draw the workshop sawtooth as smooth slopes and the library vault as a smooth curve; other roofs are flat.
Not supported:
- Café: "an orange awning of stepped cubes". No café in any panel; nearest is a small yellow flat-roofed stall (build mode).
- Umbrella: "A stepped canopy of orange cubes". No parasols; only hand-held rain umbrellas, mostly navy, smooth-domed.
Missing:
- Benches in the square are all timber, backless, with block ends; stone legs and a back only in the park.
- A1 has black hands and feet and dark ear panels; the leaf sits on a white chest plate.
Palette: right family. The tram stripe is plain orange (#D66624), the library's orange.

## Cel-shaded anime
Contradicted:
- Guild hall: "grey metal sawtooth roofs carrying skylights" (and STYLES "roof: silver-grey standing-seam metal"). Roof slopes are red-brown like the brick, each with one long blue-grey skylight; grey only in gathering. Silver metal is the library vault alone.
- Bridge: "Grey stone arches with a plain parapet". The stone is cream (#EFCFB5); the park view has a dark iron railing and lamp posts on the deck.
Not supported:
- Umbrella: "a thin vermilion edge". Plain white canopies; indigo ones at night.
- Houses: "peach, sky and mint". White to pale grey, one tan.
- Café: no café building or awning anywhere; parasols stand in front of the workshop and library.
- Great tree: "a low stone bed with shrubs". The trunk rises straight from the paving in street, build mode and night.
Missing:
- The tram carries two coral bands, at the roofline and below the windows, with navy doors.
- Planters are long, low grey concrete troughs with loose shrubs, not clipped ones.
Palette: right family. Stone greys fit the planters; bridge and paving are cream.

## Solarpunk
Contradicted:
- Umbrella: "A jade-green canopy". Cream-white canvas; no jade canopy in any sheet.
- Tram shelter: "on pale posts, with timber seating". Dark slim steel posts and glass screens, no seat visible.
Not supported:
- Bridge: "planted parapets". Plain parapets.
Missing:
- Planter: cream stone boxes are the common form; timber crates only in gathering and build mode.
- The night panel has short dark bollard lights with glowing heads.
- A1 has black hands, a brass coil neck and a hanging leaf name tag.
Palette: right family.

## Neon noir
Contradicted:
- Lamp post: "a warm globe". A tapered glass lantern in most panels; a globe only in street.
- STYLES "wall workshop: charcoal steel panels". Gable walls are vertical ribbed cladding that reads tan to brown (#8E5937 as lit), lighter than the dark frame.
Not supported:
- Café, tram shelter, umbrella: none is drawn. The only umbrellas are hand-held black rain umbrellas.
- Houses: "one thin neon line". Neon is only on the towers; houses have grey walls and navy flat roofs.
- Bench: "a warm light strip under the seat". The timber bench has a short light bar under its back rail (park); the under-edge strips are on concrete block seats (gathering).
Missing:
- A1 has a silver-white cranium plate over the black visor and a cyan cheek bar besides the ear ring; the leaf is on a white lanyard card.
Palette: right family, except the neon lines are more violet (about #B030C0) than #FF3DB8.

## Pixel art
Contradicted:
- Bench: "Two broad back planks with lit top edges". Three back planks, only the top one lit (park); the benches in the square are backless.
- Bridge: "dark iron railings". A pale stone parapet between stone posts, with black lamp posts. The iron bars are the promenade railing.
- A1: "a dark jacket with a leaf emblem". The leaf is on the backpack and the lanyard card; the jacket has a round blue-and-white shoulder patch.
Not supported:
- Café: "a red and white striped awning". None; only a plain blue awning (park) and a plain cream one (street).
- Umbrella: "A white canopy with a coloured edge". No parasols; only rain umbrellas at night.
Missing:
- Planters are brown brick boxes in street, diagonal, build mode and transit; grey stone only in park, conversation and gathering.
- STYLES "accent: red, teal and yellow" omits the recurring royal blue (#0251A4: banners, A1's scarf); teal does not appear.
Palette: right family, but brick (#F2704B) and water (#027FBF to #20A9E4) are drawn brighter than the stated hexes.
