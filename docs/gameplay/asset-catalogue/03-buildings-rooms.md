# Buildings and rooms

[Catalogue](README.md) · [Shared characteristics](CHARACTERISTICS.md) · Proposed asset kinds, 2026-09-18.

A building is a spatial container, not the service itself. These reusable premises support the existing facility IDs in the city plan. Interiors may be modular; their contents retain their own ownership.

All rows inherit the shared identity, ownership, presentation, accessibility and lifecycle characteristics where applicable. The fields below distinguish this kind. Interactions describe intended behavior, not implemented capabilities.

| ID | Kind and description | Treatment | Distinguishing characteristics | Interactions and states |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BLD-01 | **Home or apartment.** Private living space for a resident and permitted guests. | Fixed | Footprint, entrances, rooms, occupancy, plot association and visitor policy. | Enter/decorate/invite with grants; locked/open; land and residency transfer are separate. |
| BLD-02 | **Workshop and project studio.** Place for making things and collaborating; supports F02/F04. | Fixed | Work zones, equipment mounts, storage, project references and team access. | Observe public work; authorized use/edit; private project content remains private. |
| BLD-03 | **Library and reading room.** Reading, discovery and quiet shared study; supports F05. | Fixed | Shelving zones, reading seats, loan/return points and accessible routes. | Browse/read/reserve according to service permissions; physical books tracked separately. |
| BLD-04 | **Shop and market stall.** Premises displaying goods offered by authorized sellers; supports F09. | Fixed | Display slots, seller identity, counter, stock storage and trading area. | Inspect listings; buy through an explicit transaction; display is not permission to take. |
| BLD-05 | **Classroom and research room.** Space for instruction and bounded experiments; supports F06/F07. | Fixed | Seats, instructor position, presentation surfaces, experiment zones and booking. | Attend/reserve/use equipment; service access separate from room entry. |
| BLD-06 | **Civic hall and assembly room.** Shared space for meetings and city deliberation; supports F13. | Fixed | Speaking positions, seats, accessible routes, session limits and display surfaces. | Join/observe sessions; speaking and voting rights are independent. |
| BLD-07 | **Service station.** Reusable clinic, safety, rescue or utility premises; supports F14–F17. | Fixed | Service role, dispatch/access zones, equipment bays and public/private rooms. | Inspect authorized public status; simulation services do not grant operational authority. |
| BLD-08 | **Transit terminal and depot.** Passenger interchange and vehicle service premises; supports F18. | Fixed | Platforms, entrances, routes, waiting space, storage and boarding interfaces. | Route inspection/boarding; separate passenger, staff and maintenance access. |
| BLD-09 | **Gallery, museum and observatory.** Space for exhibits, history and approved live work projections; supports F03/F19/F20. | Fixed | Exhibit anchors, viewing positions, source references, freshness and visibility. | Observe/inspect; service-backed requests require their own authorization. |
| BLD-10 | **Rooftop terrace and courtyard.** Outdoor room for social use, gardens and views. | Fixed | Usable bounds, access route, railing, seating mounts, shade and occupancy. | Visit/decorate/host where granted; preserve edge protection and emergency routes. |
