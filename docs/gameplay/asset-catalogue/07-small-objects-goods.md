# Small objects and goods

[Catalogue](README.md) · [Shared characteristics](CHARACTERISTICS.md) · Proposed asset kinds, 2026-09-18.

Most of these objects are intended to become portable over time. Begin with meaningful books, tools and project artifacts; ordinary decorations may initially be scenery while retaining a path to the same object model.

All rows inherit the shared identity, ownership, presentation, accessibility and lifecycle characteristics where applicable. The fields below distinguish this kind. Interactions describe intended behavior, not implemented capabilities.

| ID | Kind and description | Treatment | Distinguishing characteristics | Interactions and states |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OBJ-01 | **Book and document.** Readable object with cover, pages and a content reference. | Portable | Edition/content reference, cover, readable layout, loan status and shelf size. | Read/carry/shelve/borrow/return; physical ownership does not grant restricted content. |
| OBJ-02 | **Notebook and sketchbook.** Personal or shared writable record. | Portable | Page/content reference, author/editor grants, cover customization and privacy. | Read/write/carry/store; selling the shell does not transfer project or document rights. |
| OBJ-03 | **Project artifact and prototype.** A tangible outcome of a maker activity. | Portable | Creator, project reference, version, dimensions, inspectable features and provenance. | Inspect/display/carry/transfer; a live project remains under its separate authority. |
| OBJ-04 | **Material stock and component.** Wood, fabric, wire, fastener or assembly input. | Portable | Material/type, quantity unit, stackability, compatibility and batch condition. | Store/split/combine/consume later; stack only equivalent fungible units. |
| OBJ-05 | **Cup, bottle and vessel.** Reusable container for drinks or decorative contents. | Portable | Vessel capacity, lid, fill state, surface support and distinct contents. | Carry/place/store; later fill/empty; selling a vessel specifies contents. |
| OBJ-06 | **Food and consumable supply.** Item that may be used up through a defined action. | Portable | Portion/quantity, use effect, packaging, stackability and optional freshness. | Store/use/transfer; no hunger or spoilage simulation assumed. |
| OBJ-07 | **Ornament, sculpture and souvenir.** Personal decoration or keepsake. | Portable | Silhouette, creator, material, support, provenance and customization. | Inspect/place/store/gift/sell where eligible; uniqueness does not imply scarcity pricing. |
| OBJ-08 | **Picture, poster and frame.** Display object containing an image or approved text. | Portable | Media reference, orientation, frame, mounting and viewing permissions. | Mount/inspect/change content with rights; media access separate from frame ownership. |
| OBJ-09 | **Key, pass and ticket representation.** Visible representation of an access entitlement. | Portable | Issuer/reference, validity, permitted action, holder binding and transfer eligibility. | Inspect/use; copying, gifting or stealing its mesh never copies the entitlement. |
| OBJ-10 | **Parcel and delivery package.** Tracked goods bundle addressed to a recipient. | Portable | Sender, recipient, contents manifest, package size, seal and delivery state. | Collect/carry/deliver/open with grants; carrier possession is not goods ownership. |
| OBJ-11 | **Award, badge and collectible.** Displayable recognition or collection item. | Portable | Issuer, award criteria reference, display slot, provenance and transfer policy. | Display/inspect; earned identity recognition may be non-transferable even if frame is sellable. |
| OBJ-12 | **Loose stationery and everyday clutter.** Paper, folders, desk accessories and minor possessions. | Portable | Subtype, size, support, stack/group policy and optional content reference. | Initially scenery candidates; later pickup/store/arrange without independent ongoing simulation. |
