# Tools and equipment

[Catalogue](README.md) · [Shared characteristics](CHARACTERISTICS.md) · Proposed asset kinds, 2026-09-18.

Tools expose specific capabilities and compatible targets. Their appearance must not imply an operational connection to real infrastructure or unlimited agent permissions.

All rows inherit the shared identity, ownership, presentation, accessibility and lifecycle characteristics where applicable. The fields below distinguish this kind. Interactions describe intended behavior, not implemented capabilities.

| ID | Kind and description | Treatment | Distinguishing characteristics | Interactions and states |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TOOL-01 | **Hand tool.** Reusable screwdriver, hammer, wrench or similar work implement. | Portable | Tool subtype, grip, target compatibility, use animation and optional condition. | Pick up/equip/use/return; valid task target required; not universal world destruction. |
| TOOL-02 | **Measuring and inspection tool.** Ruler, gauge or scanner for inspecting a project. | Portable | Measurement type, units, target range, display and precision representation. | Equip/inspect; distinguish simulation measurements from real service data. |
| TOOL-03 | **Writing and art tool.** Pen, brush or drawing implement. | Portable | Grip, medium, palette, compatible surface and optional consumable supply. | Equip/write/draw on authorized surfaces; content permissions remain enforced. |
| TOOL-04 | **Computer and terminal.** Physical entry point to permitted information and work services. | Movable | Screen, keyboard/input anchors, session scope, service references and power state. | Inspect/sign in/use/sign out; machine possession does not grant another user's session. |
| TOOL-05 | **Fabrication machine.** Printer, cutter or assembly station producing authored outputs. | Movable | Work envelope, input/output slots, recipes, job capacity and cancellation policy. | Queue/start/collect under grants; idle/running/blocked; outputs have explicit ownership. |
| TOOL-06 | **Repair station.** Equipment for restoring compatible objects. | Movable | Accepted items, required supplies, work slot, outcome and operator permission. | Submit/repair/retrieve; borrowed-item modification requires owner authorization. |
| TOOL-07 | **Camera and recording device.** Portable device for permitted photos or recordings. | Portable | Lens/view mode, recording indicator, storage destination and access. | Aim/capture/review; private spaces/content require explicit capture rules. |
| TOOL-08 | **Projector and presentation equipment.** Displays approved material to an audience. | Movable | Mount, projection area, source reference, controls and captions. | Present/pause/stop; audience visibility checked independently of presenter ownership. |
| TOOL-09 | **Charging dock and supply station.** Restores charge or supplies to compatible equipment. | Movable | Mounts, compatible connectors, slots, rate representation and access. | Dock/replenish/release; reserved/charging/ready; costs belong to service rules. |
