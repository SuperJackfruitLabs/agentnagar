# Streamline retro future

Concept art, not gameplay or performance evidence. [All styles](../README.md).

Original generation prompts for this style were not saved; revision prompts are now saved beside the sheets. For the original exploration, the frame treatment follows the [candidate brief](../CANDIDATE-STYLES.md).

## Style description

A painted, optimistic city of aerodynamic forms. Cream enamel, teal and orange accents, chrome trim, and glass canopies define the look, with finned towers and airships in the skyline. Streamlined trams on elevated guideways are the most recognizable element.

Buildings are rounded volumes with horizontal banding, porthole windows, and curved glass fronts. Interiors are bright and tidy, with chrome fixtures and curved furniture. People are painted in a clean illustrative style; the city agent is a white and teal humanoid robot with a dark visor, though the mobile panel shows a human portrait instead.

Large views read as pale rounded blocks along tree-lined boulevards, with the tram and fountain square as landmarks. Street scenes show cafes, banners, and the tram at ground level. Close-ups hold together because the enamel palette is consistent across furniture, vehicles, and interfaces.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve rounded silhouettes, the cream and teal palette, and the tram at every tier. Lower tiers would drop chrome reflections, airships, and crowds; higher tiers could add enamel gloss and glass transparency. The skyline drifts toward a generic science-fiction metropolis that overlaps with the Art Deco candidate, and three sheets crop the footer caption.
