# Art Nouveau botanical

Concept art, not gameplay or performance evidence. [All styles](../README.md).

Generation prompts for this style were not saved; the frame treatment follows the [candidate brief](../CANDIDATE-STYLES.md).

## Style description

A garden city of curved iron and glass, where plant forms shape the architecture, furniture, and lettering. Sage green, cream, brass, and lavender dominate, with teal water and palms placing the setting in a warm climate. Floral borders frame every panel.

Arched greenhouse halls, spired towers, and tiled domes make up the skyline; the workshop is a glass shed on the dock. Interiors carry leaf motifs into lamps, bedding, rugs, and tram seats. People wear botanical prints, and the agent is a white figure with green and gold leaf inlays.

The map is legible with named districts, but the diagonal view is dense enough that landmarks blur together. Street and close-up scenes read well, with ornament working as wayfinding.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve the whiplash silhouettes, the green and brass palette, and the mosaic paving at every quality tier. Lower tiers would simplify ironwork to flat outlines and remove climbing plants; higher tiers could add glass reflections and inlay. The generated sheets put ornament on every surface, so the city reads as a palace garden rather than a maker district; the creating sheet also moves its labels to the panel bottoms.
