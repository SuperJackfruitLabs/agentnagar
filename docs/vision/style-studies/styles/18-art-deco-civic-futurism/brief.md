# Art Deco civic futurism — brief

Original exploration intent; historical architectural details may be superseded
by shared comparison rules and the dated reviews. This migration does not select
a production style.

A formal, symmetrical public city of stepped towers, sunburst reliefs, and fluted pilasters. Midnight blue, brass, and cream stone set the palette, with blue and gold tiled domes and warm window light. The map includes a legend.

Buildings are monumental with tall arched entrances; trams run on the boulevard. Interiors use brass fittings, navy upholstery, and marble. People wear hats and long coats in the transit and night panels, which reads as a period setting. The agent is a cream and navy android with a sash and sunburst emblem.

Large views hold together through symmetry and the central tower. Street scenes are grand plazas with fountains; close-ups are warmly lit and detailed. Interfaces are dark panels with brass trim.
