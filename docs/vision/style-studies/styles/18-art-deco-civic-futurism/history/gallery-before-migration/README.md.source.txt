# Art Deco civic futurism

Concept art, not gameplay or performance evidence. [All styles](../README.md).

Original generation prompts for this style were not saved; revision prompts are now saved beside the sheets. For the original exploration, the frame treatment follows the [candidate brief](../CANDIDATE-STYLES.md).

## Style description

A formal, symmetrical public city of stepped towers, sunburst reliefs, and fluted pilasters. Midnight blue, brass, and cream stone set the palette, with blue and gold tiled domes and warm window light. The map includes a legend.

Buildings are monumental with tall arched entrances; trams run on the boulevard. Interiors use brass fittings, navy upholstery, and marble. People wear hats and long coats in the transit and night panels, which reads as a period setting. The agent is a cream and navy android with a sash and sunburst emblem.

Large views hold together through symmetry and the central tower. Street scenes are grand plazas with fountains; close-ups are warmly lit and detailed. Interfaces are dark panels with brass trim.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve the stepped silhouettes, the sunburst motif, and the midnight and brass palette at every quality tier. Lower tiers would remove relief detail and reflections; higher tiers could add metallic highlights and window glow. The generated sheets are the most photorealistic of the set and lean on 1930s costume, so decide how stylized figures should be first; the living sheet footer also misspells "concept".
