## Prompt

```text
Create ONE high-resolution landscape 2x2 four-view game environment art-direction sheet for an original large contemporary riverfront maker metropolis. STYLE: ARCHITECTURAL REALISM, natural light, believable human scale, subtle weathering, realistic red brick concrete glass steel wood and asphalt, physically plausible trees, authentic modern city density. This is a realistic environment concept, not a photographed real location. No cartoon proportions, no voxel cubes, no painted ink outlines, no pastel toy-town style, no magical futuristic holograms. Four large equal panels labeled exactly MAP, TOP-DOWN, DIAGONAL, STREET in narrow neutral margins. Shared geography: river west, bridge north, a public tree square, industrial brick workshop west of square, modern glass-and-stone library east, street-running tram boulevard south, dense blocks and downtown towers north. TOP LEFT MAP: strict flat 2D professional urban navigation map, precise road and transit lines, flat gray footprint polygons, green parks, blue river, readable restrained map design, NO extrusions, NO facades, NO 3D perspective. TOP RIGHT TOP-DOWN: exactly vertical nadir aerial view, orthographic roofscape, skylights and roof gardens with real trees and pavement seen straight down, no horizon, NO side walls of buildings visible, NO tilt. BOTTOM LEFT DIAGONAL: expansive oblique aerial city view at 45 degrees, many blocks, taller downtown, river and bridge, coherent realistic city planning and metropolitan scale, foreground workshop square discernible. BOTTOM RIGHT STREET: eye-height walking perspective inside tree square, people sitting and walking, a tram visible beyond, detailed workshop entrance, modern library, distant towers, real-world proportions and quiet lived-in quality. Neutral sunny afternoon consistently across views. Sharp scene readability without cinematic blur, moderate natural saturation. Thin off-white gutters, clean small headings, no dense UI, no brand names. Footer 'Architectural realism — concept art'. An art direction target only, do not add performance claims or FPS. Ensure each perspective is visibly different and accurately matches its label.
```
