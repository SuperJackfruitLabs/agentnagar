Dept. 02

Art department

Intent from the brief

A warm city that resembles a handmade stop-motion set. Rounded sculpted forms, slight irregularities, and tactile surfaces give buildings and residents an approachable personality.

Architecture should retain useful distinctions despite its softness: workshops, homes, and public buildings need different silhouettes and entrances. Interiors can combine sculpted furniture with textile-like details and carefully arranged props.

Sculpting rules

  1. Soft but distinct. Rounded forms, yet the sawtooth Workshop, the vaulted Library and the planted towers keep separate silhouettes and entrances.
  2. Show the hand. Slight irregularities, knit and crochet textures and thumb-pressed surfaces, never factory-smooth.
  3. Faces carry it. Big expressive eyes, clear gestures and a shared handmade look for people and agents alike.
  4. Model at the overview, intimate at the street. The city reads as a large physical model; homes and streets feel close and cosy.
  5. Cadence is a separate choice. The look does not require a low frame rate; motion is chosen for responsiveness and comfort.

Clay tubs colours sampled from the concept sheets

    Materials and texture

    Modelling clay with visible tool marks and thumbprints; knit and crochet for hats, scarves and cardigans; textile cushions and throws; wooden benches and props; glossy water with sculpted ripples; small warm practical lights on the set at night.

    Typography

    Fredoka, rounded like a rolled coil

    Nunito for reading, soft and open

    Patrick Hand for crew notes and tape labels

    The sheets themselves use a plain bold sans for panel captions and a rounded sans on the phone and kiosk UI.

      Dept. 04

      Puppet department

      In the clay sheets A1 wears a teal knit pompom hat over dark curls, a chunky mustard scarf and a blue jacket, with large eyes and rosy cheeks. The leaf badge sits on the jacket; the repair record keeps that look in every close view and in the phone portrait.

      A1 sculpts a small model of the sawtooth Workshop at the bench, reads at home with a sleeping cat, talks under the shade tree with a grey-curled resident holding a leaf mug, and helps her at the library kiosk. Clay faces are the style's biggest draw: the Conversation panel is where it is most appealing.

      Puppets not yet sculpted Guild residents

      The concept sheets do not show any Guild resident; the other puppets are unnamed residents. Proposed roles only.
