## Prompt

```text
ONE landscape high-resolution four-panel 2x2 art-direction sheet for a vast walkable maker city, STYLE: CRISP CEL-SHADED ANIME GAME ENVIRONMENT. Bold clean two-tone shadow shapes, fine selective outlines, vivid blue sky, sunlit warm whites, indigo shadows, vermilion accents, bright graphic foliage, stylized anime human residents in casual contemporary clothing. Polished original anime city, no copyrighted characters, no photorealistic surface noise, no crosshatching, no voxel shapes, no clay materials. Buildings contemporary Japanese-influenced urban architecture: compact mixed-use shopfronts, a modern maker school/workshop with exposed structural frames, glass library with colorful fins, tram lanes, rooftop terraces, taller downtown and distant green hills. Shared geography: river west, bridge north, central square with tree, workshop west of square, library east, tram boulevard south, downtown north. Four equally spacious panels with small clean labels MAP, TOP-DOWN, DIAGONAL, STREET. MAP upper left is an absolutely flat 2D cartographic navigation map of many districts, simple footprint shapes and colored transit lines, no perspective or building heights. TOP-DOWN upper right is exact vertical overhead roofscape view with anime flat-shaded roofs, no horizon or tilted camera. DIAGONAL lower left is wide elevated isometric cityscape showing many blocks, river bridge and towers, city-scale not isolated diorama. STREET lower right is at human eye-level walking into the square, stylized anime residents, dramatic clear architectural perspective, workshop and library entrances, tram and skyline visible. Same core landmarks across views. Strong authored shapes and beautiful purposeful color, limited cel shadow steps, NO realistic texture maps. Narrow light gutters, clear panel labels. Footer '06 · Cel-shaded anime city — concept art'. No extra explanatory paragraphs, brands, dense game UI, FPS claims or depth-of-field blur.
```
