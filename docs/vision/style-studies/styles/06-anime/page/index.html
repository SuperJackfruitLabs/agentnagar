    The style

    A cel-shaded city with expressive characters, composed color, and selective outlines. Warm whites, blues, indigo, and vermilion support lively daylight scenes. Limited shadow steps create an illustrated feel while architecture retains convincing spatial depth.

    Buildings can combine contemporary urban forms with distinctive roofs, balconies, shopfronts, and transit infrastructure. Interiors should communicate character through layout and selected possessions.

    Principles

    1. Two or three shadow steps. Flat light, one cel shadow, an occasional highlight; no soft airbrushing.
    2. Selective outlines. Ink where silhouettes matter (people, roofs, the tram); background detail can drop its lines.
    3. Composed colour. Warm whites and blues carry the day, indigo anchors, vermilion accents: the tram stripe, a sweater, an umbrella.
    4. Silhouette first at district scale. Sawtooth Workshop, vaulted Library and planted towers read by shape and colour blocks.
    5. Same language in every weather. Night and rain keep the cel shading instead of switching to a realistic treatment.

      Materials and texture

      Flat-painted brick and zinc with crisp shadow shapes, glass as a single highlight stripe, leaf clusters in three tones, wet paving drawn as streaked reflections at night. The sheets drift into painterly foliage and sky in places.

      Typography

      Zen Old Mincho for titles and chapter cards, Nunito for dialogue and UI. The sheets' own UI uses a clean rounded sans on the phone and kiosk.

      Agents in this style

      In the anime sheets A1 is an original design: indigo hair in a messy bun with a hair tie, blue eyes, a white jacket over a blue shirt, a backpack, a leaf patch on the sleeve and a "City Agent A1" badge on the chest. The phone card repeats her portrait with an explicit leaf A1 marker.

      She works at the Workshop bench on a small wheeled robot, talks with a bearded resident in a green jacket on Tree Square, points out the Workshop from a rooftop and helps at the Library kiosk.

      Guild residents not depicted in the sheets

      The sheets show no Guild resident; everyone else is an unnamed resident. These are proposed public roles only.
