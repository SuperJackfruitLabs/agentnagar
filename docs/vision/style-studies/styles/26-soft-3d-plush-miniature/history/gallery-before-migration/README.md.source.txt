# Soft 3D plush miniature

Concept art, not gameplay or performance evidence. [All styles](../README.md).

Generation prompts for this style were not saved; the frame treatment follows the [candidate brief](../CANDIDATE-STYLES.md).

## Style description

A riverfront city built as a felt and knit miniature set, lit like a tabletop diorama. Cream, sage green, denim blue, warm wood, and a coral tram carry the palette. Trees are pompom masses, cobbles are stitched, and posters are embroidered patches.

Buildings are padded volumes with visible seams: clock towers, domes, a workshop with a gear sign, and a library with an open-book emblem. Interiors use knit sofas, felt laptops, and quilted blankets. People are round-headed dolls with button eyes and knit hats; the agent is a white felt robot with a blue smiling screen face.

Large views should keep the diorama feel through soft depth of field and simple stitched shapes. Street scenes carry the strongest material identity in bunting, striped awnings, and felt lamps. Close-ups read best, since fibre texture on faces and clothing is where the style is most distinct.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve padded silhouettes, stitched edges, and doll proportions at every quality tier. Lower tiers would flatten fibre texture and drop soft shadows; higher tiers could add fuzz, embroidery detail, and diorama lighting. Watch scale: at map and diagonal distance the generated panels read as a generic painted miniature, and the mobile and AR tabletop panels show a photographic human hand.
