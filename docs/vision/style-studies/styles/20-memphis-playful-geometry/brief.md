# Memphis playful geometry — brief

Original exploration intent; historical architectural details may be superseded
by shared comparison rules and the dated reviews. This migration does not select
a production style.

A high-energy city of arches, cylinders, tilted blocks, and ball-topped towers on a white ground. Pink, yellow, teal, cobalt, and black share the palette, with terrazzo speckle and squiggle patterns on almost every surface. Section labels sit on colored chips.

Buildings are stacked geometric volumes with patterned facades; yellow trams cross the plaza. Interiors continue the pattern into sofas, tables, cups, and even the cat. People wear bold prints, and the agent is a white robot with color blocks, confetti dots, and a smiling screen face.

Large views stay readable because the river and green squares break up the pattern, and the map names its districts. Street and close-up scenes are busy but cheerful. Interfaces use pastel pill buttons.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve the primitive silhouettes, the five-color palette, and the speckle on landmarks at every quality tier. Lower tiers would keep pattern only on hero buildings and clothing; higher tiers could animate confetti and add soft shadow. The generated sheets pattern buildings, props, clothing, and pets alike, so navigation cues are hard to separate from decoration; test route visibility and pattern stability in motion early.
