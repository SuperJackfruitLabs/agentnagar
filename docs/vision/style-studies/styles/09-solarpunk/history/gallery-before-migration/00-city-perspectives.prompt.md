# City perspectives — original generation prompt

Exact prompt recovered from the original image-generation call in the design conversation on 2026-09-18. This is the prompt used for [archived original 00-city-perspectives.png](../../sheets/00-city-perspectives/r001/image.png), not a reconstruction. No reference image was supplied in this call.

## Prompt

```text
Create ONE high-resolution wide landscape 2x2 concept art sheet for an explorable SOLARPUNK RETRO-FUTURIST MAKER METROPOLIS. Four equal spacious panels with clean labels MAP, TOP-DOWN, DIAGONAL, STREET. Art direction: 1960s optimistic future reimagined as clean stylized 3D game art, sculptural streamlined white ceramic buildings, rounded balconies, gold/brass ribs, turquoise glass, terracotta walkways, huge planted terraces and roof gardens, solar canopies, elegant suspended tram, expressive simplified people in casual clothes, rich teal jade coral and cream. Architecture radically different from conventional brick city: sweeping curves, terraced towers, circular library atrium, arched maker pavilions, light pedestrian bridges, city-wide ecological infrastructure. Optimistic inhabited human-scale spaces, no sterile empty science fiction, no holograms, no dystopia, no photorealism. Shared geography: river west, bridge north, central tree square, maker workshop west, circular public library east, tram boulevard south, dense sculptural downtown north. MAP upper left is a completely FLAT 2D graphic plan with curved footprint shapes, botanical-green park patches, blue water and colored transport lines, no perspective or 3D buildings. TOP-DOWN upper right: camera exactly vertically above rooftops, solar canopies, green terraces and square, no horizon. DIAGONAL lower left: wide 45-degree city overview with many districts and futuristic terraced skyscrapers, river bridge and lush public spaces. STREET lower right: eye-level in the maker square beneath a generous solar shade canopy, people walking and collaborating, tram in background, workshop on left and library right, curved skyline. Full city scale and genuine street life. Crisp attractive stylized rendering, broad deliberate material shapes, controlled detail. Narrow cream gutters and footer '09 · Solarpunk city — concept art'. No dense UI, brands, performance claims or depth-of-field blur.
```
