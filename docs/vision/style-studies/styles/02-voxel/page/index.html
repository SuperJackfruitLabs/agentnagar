The selected style for first-scene assets

On 2026-09-22 Rakesh selected Voxel for building the first scene assets and authorised a local Blender → GLB → validation → Godot work-bay experiment. That authorised the local experiment only: full Workshop integration, publication, final engine selection and acceptance of all fourteen character designs remain separate decisions. Selection does not imply review approval of these sheets.

Since RD18 on 2026-09-24 the city is being built so its mechanics work in any style. Low-poly (11) and pixel art (08) prove that first; Voxel follows as the third style pack in the Godot city client.
