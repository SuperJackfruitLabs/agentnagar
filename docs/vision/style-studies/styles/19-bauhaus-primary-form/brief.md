# Bauhaus primary-form — brief

Original exploration intent; historical architectural details may be superseded
by shared comparison rules and the dated reviews. This migration does not select
a production style.

A flat, systematic city of white modular blocks with red, blue, and yellow panels on a cream ground. Black line accents and sans-serif labels give every sheet the look of a diagram. A red disc on the workshop roof is a landmark from every view.

Buildings are rectilinear volumes with a domed library and yellow trams. Interiors use white walls, primary-colored furniture, and pegboard tool walls. People wear color-blocked clothing; the agent is a white robot with primary panels and a black face with glowing eyes.

Large views stay readable because the palette is strict and the shapes are few, and the map is a labelled schematic. Close-ups keep the same flat shading. Interfaces use circle, square, and triangle icons for Browse, Book, and Ask, which gives non-color cues for free.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve the three primary colors, the white volumes, and the geometric icon set at every quality tier. Lower tiers would reduce trees to discs and remove crowds; higher tiers could add soft shadows and material grain. This is the most consistent set of the five, but the strict kit could make districts interchangeable, and the generated city reads as a generic modern skyline.
