## Style description

Carved marks and heavy black ink over ivory paper, with a small palette of rust, olive, muted blue, and warm ochre. Every surface carries visible tool texture, and contrast comes from ink density rather than color.

Brick and timber buildings, a columned library, iron dock cranes, and arched bridges form the district. Interiors are dark-lined but warm, with patterned quilts, tool walls, and plants. People have inked hair and clothing with simple lit faces; the agent is a pale robot with a green scarf and a black visor face.

The map is a dense carved diagram that stays legible. Diagonal and street views pack in detail and look busy at reduced size. Night and rain is the strongest scene: lit windows and wet paving cut cleanly through the dark. Interfaces are black frames with ivory labels.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve the black keyline, carved texture, and small palette at every tier. Lower tiers would reduce mark density on distant surfaces; higher tiers could keep full texture and ink bleed. The skyline is a generic modern high-rise city rather than a riverfront maker town, and the diagonal view loses figures inside the hatching.
