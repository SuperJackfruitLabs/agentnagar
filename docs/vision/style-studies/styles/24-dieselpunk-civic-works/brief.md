# Dieselpunk civic works — brief

Original exploration intent; historical architectural details may be superseded
by shared comparison rules and the dated reviews. This migration does not select
a production style.

An industrious brick and iron city with warm light in every window. Riveted steel, exposed girders, brass fittings, chain hoists, and copper domes set the identity. Steel gray, ochre, brick red, and amber lamplight carry the palette, with airships and arched iron bridges in most skylines.

Buildings are brick halls with iron truss roofs, glass-vaulted stations, and domed civic towers. Interiors show machinery on chains, drafting tables, leather trunks, and wood-panelled trams. People wear overalls, flat caps, goggles, and long coats; the city agent is a riveted metal humanoid with round lamp eyes, a coat, and a ledger.

Large views read as a dense historic downtown with domes, chimneys, and elevated tram lines. Street scenes show lamplit plazas, monuments, and signposts. Close-ups carry warmth through brass, leather, and lamp glow, and the kiosk, phone, and tabletop reuse the same materials.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve brick and iron silhouettes, ochre and steel colors, and the tram at every tier. Lower tiers would simplify rivets, girders, and crowds; higher tiers could add rain reflections and moving machinery. Period costume, a heroic worker statue, and civic monuments push the city toward a 1930s setting rather than a contemporary one, the concern the candidate brief asked this study to check.
