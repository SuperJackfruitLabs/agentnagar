# City perspectives — original generation prompt

Exact prompt recovered from the original image-generation call in the design conversation on 2026-09-18. This is the prompt used for [00-city-perspectives.png](00-city-perspectives.png), not a reconstruction. No reference image was supplied in this call.

## Prompt

```text
Generate ONE large landscape 2x2 game art-direction contact sheet, original contemporary maker city, STYLE: GRAPHIC MINIMALIST 3D. Four spacious equal panels labeled 'MAP', 'TOP-DOWN', 'DIAGONAL', 'STREET'. Style across all panels: graphic coral navy cream mint palette, simple clean geometry, deliberately flat-shaded surfaces, minimal textures, crisp silhouettes, geometric trees and abstract but expressive humanoid residents, elegant visual clarity. Not realistic, not voxel, no ink outlines. Same city design across views: river bends along west edge, bridge north, central square with round tree planter, maker workshop west of square, library east, tram boulevard south, dense mid-rise neighborhoods and taller downtown to north. Original architecture: cubic modern workshops, arcades and flat roof gardens, no repeated Mediterranean village. MAP panel must be a STRICTLY FLAT 2D vector city plan with simple solid building FOOTPRINTS, lines for roads and transit, circles for trees, blue river, no extruded buildings, no facades, no shadows, no perspective. TOP-DOWN panel is exactly vertical aerial view of actual 3D rooftops and square with subtle cast shadows; no horizon, no oblique view. DIAGONAL panel is orthographic isometric bird's-eye view showing multiple populated blocks, river and a metropolitan skyline of simple geometric towers. STREET panel is true pedestrian eye level in the square looking toward tram boulevard and distant towers, nearby people and trees at human scale, not aerial. Keep important buildings and relative layout consistent. Each panel genuinely uses its named camera. Thin cream gutters, small clean dark labels above panels. Small footer 'Graphic city — concept art'. No extra captions, logos, UI, fake statistics, depth-of-field blur, textured realistic surfaces or photorealistic lighting. High-quality distinctive art direction for a cross-device explorable city. Wide landscape image.
```
