Neon noir

11:48 p.m. The rain had the boulevard to itself. Amber lamps along the tram line, a cyan glow in the library dome, three towers wearing magenta on their shoulders. Somewhere in the Workshop, the lights were still on.

I was hired to find out whether a city could look like this and still be easy to live in. What follows is the file: four photographs, a description of the look, the people and agents who turned up, and the parts that don't add up yet.

For the record: the photographs are concept studies, not game captures. Nobody in them has been built.
