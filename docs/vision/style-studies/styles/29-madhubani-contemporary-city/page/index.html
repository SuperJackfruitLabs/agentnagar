Agentnagar style study 29

Madhubani-inspired contemporary city

The reference district told as a wall of story panels. Every shape is closed by a double line; pattern lives in the borders; the ground inside each panel stays plain so people and words stand out.

This is a Madhubani-inspired study. It borrows a drawing grammar and makes no claim of cultural authenticity; the brief makes collaboration with artists knowledgeable in the Mithila tradition a precondition for any production use.
