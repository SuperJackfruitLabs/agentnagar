## Iteration review

Overview passes 1–2 reduced inherited obliqueness; pass 3 moved STREET tram forward and simplified roof views; pass 4 restored N arrows. Living pass 2 removed contradictory panoramas, pass 3 corrected visible roof counts. Creating passes 2–3 removed medieval background and aligned captions. Interface pass 2 corrected rooftop but accidentally added a second AR waterway; pass 3 restored AR. Pass 4 improved captions but clipped the phone and lost External viewer; pass 5 corrected those regressions. A sixth isolated rooftop-only pass was requested by independent review; it overshot to two roof bays and softened the pixel treatment, so it was not promoted. A seventh isolated pass added exactly one bay to that two-bay intermediate, producing the required three attached roof slopes while retaining the phone and AR fixes; pass 7 was promoted.

## Residual issues

Full compliance is not claimed. The final rooftop workshop now shows three attached long roof slopes. Repeated targeted generations softened some pixel edges and background detail relative to the original; the image is a direction reference, not a production sprite atlas. Small map and roof views retain pictorial roofs rather than perfectly flat symbols/orthographic geometry; some façade-edge slivers remain. Some experience views show only two clearly exposed downtown towers, with trees/cropping obscuring the intended third. Mobile A1 identity is clear but the tiny avatar badge is not reliably readable. Typography size, outer matte, gutter widths and exact 32/44 px strips are not uniform or pixel-verified. Transit changed from a passenger interior to boarding; this still shows the shared transit task.
