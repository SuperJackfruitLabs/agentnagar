# City perspectives — original generation prompt

Exact prompt recovered from the original image-generation call in the design conversation on 2026-09-18. This is the prompt used for [archived original 00-city-perspectives.png](../../sheets/00-city-perspectives/r001/image.png), not a reconstruction. No reference image was supplied in this call.

## Prompt

```text
Generate ONE landscape high-resolution 2x2 city game art-direction sheet. FOUR EQUAL PANELS labeled MAP, TOP-DOWN, DIAGONAL, STREET. STYLE: AUTHENTIC CRISP PIXEL ART, deliberately visible pixel clusters, limited rich 32-to-64-color palette, controlled dithering, hard pixel edges, 1990s-inspired pixel city craft at refined modern quality. No smooth gradients, no antialiased linework, no watercolor, no photorealistic textures, NO 3D voxel cubes: this is raster pixel art, not Minecraft. Warm peach brick, deep indigo, teal water, emerald trees, amber windows. Contemporary maker metropolis, river west, bridge north, workshop west of central tree square, library east, tram street south, dense neighborhoods and downtown north. MAP upper left: truly flat low-resolution pixel navigation map, clear road/rail lines and footprint blocks, park patches, blue river, no extruded buildings. TOP-DOWN upper right: straight vertical 2D game-like overhead city scene showing rooftops, square, small pixel character sprites, trees and tracks, no side perspective. DIAGONAL lower left: extensive isometric pixel city of many blocks, precise roof and facade pixel work, skyline of tall buildings, bridge and river. STREET lower right: true human-eye-level pixel-art adventure-game scene of the square, nearby pixel characters and shopfronts, perspective street receding to skyline; not isometric, not side-scrolling. Same original city landmarks across views. Emphasize excellent pixel composition and readability. Thin neutral gutters and simple pixel typography. Footer '08 · Pixel-art city — concept art'. No brand names, dense UI or benchmark claims. Ensure city feels expansive and populated. Pixel grid visible and deliberate even in the street view; no smooth 3D render masquerading as pixel art.
```
