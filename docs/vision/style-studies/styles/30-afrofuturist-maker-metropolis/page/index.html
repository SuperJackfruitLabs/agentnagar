Style study 30 · maker festival programme

Afrofuturist maker metropolis

Copper lattice towers, patterned facades and a great tree shading the civic square. Seven sets, one reference district. The rhythm is visual only; nothing plays sound.

An Afrofuturist-inspired study: it draws on a visual grammar and makes no claim of cultural authenticity. The brief makes a specific creative partnership and regional reference a precondition for any production use.
