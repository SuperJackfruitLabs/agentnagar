# Afrofuturist maker metropolis

Concept art, not gameplay or performance evidence. [All styles](../README.md).

Generation prompts for this style were not saved; the frame treatment follows the [candidate brief](../CANDIDATE-STYLES.md).

## Style description

A dense riverfront metropolis of copper lattice towers, patterned facades, and shaded civic plazas. Deep plum, copper, teal, ochre, and indigo form the palette, with geometric textile patterns repeated on walls, seats, and clothing. Solar panels, brass filigree, and a great tree at the central square give the district its identity.

Buildings are tall tapered towers with patterned cladding, tree-shaped shade canopies, cable-stayed bridges, and a domed library with lattice screens. Workshops hold 3D printers and a brass solar sphere. Interiors layer woven textiles over low furniture. People wear headwraps, printed jackets, and gold jewellery; the agent is a near-human figure with a gold collar and glowing blue ear rings.

Large views should read as a skyline of patterned spires with the river and tram as clear lines. Street and gathering scenes depend on canopy shade, patterned planters, and the tree. Close-ups should keep textile pattern on the kiosk, phone, and tabletop.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve tower silhouettes, the copper-and-teal palette, and geometric pattern on key surfaces at every tier. Lower tiers would simplify lattice detail and drop reflections; higher tiers could add metal specular and pattern density. A specific creative partnership and regional reference are a precondition for any production use. The generated sheets show the strongest realism drift of the set: characters and lighting are near photographic, and the diagonal view becomes a generic glass skyline with the maker district reduced to the foreground.
