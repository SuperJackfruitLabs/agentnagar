Set the district

Match the reference: river on the west, bridge to the north, tram along the south.

  • Select a tile, then select another to swap them. Or drag one onto another.
  • Select a chosen tile again, or press R, to turn it. Shift+R turns it back.
  • Arrow keys move between tiles; Enter or Space selects.
  • A tile is upright when its small blue maker's mark sits top left.
