One large landscape 2x2 concept sheet, four equal panels labeled 'MAP', 'TOP-DOWN', 'DIAGONAL', 'STREET', all depicting one contemporary maker metropolis in a radically distinctive HAND-DRAWN INK AND WATERCOLOR / GRAPHIC NOVEL aesthetic. Ivory paper ground, expressive black pen outlines, loose architectural linework, restrained crosshatching, muted rust ochre sage and teal washes, deliberate paper texture. Every panel visibly drawn by an architectural illustrator, never photorealistic, never polished realistic 3D, never voxel. Architecture: lively modern industrial brick workshops with sawtooth roofs and tall windows, a public library in a repurposed warehouse, balconies, steel footbridges, tram street, river, rooftop gardens, taller downtown beyond. Same city geography in all panels: river along west, bridge north, workshop west of central tree square, library east, tram boulevard south, dense downtown north. TOP LEFT MAP: exact flat orthographic 2D urban planning map of districts, blue watercolor river, black fine road lines, ink footprint shapes, green park washes, small map symbols; NO perspective, NO building elevations or extrusions. TOP RIGHT TOP-DOWN: camera pointed exactly 90 degrees straight down, a roof plan view showing actual roofs, skylights, trees from above and paths; no visible vertical building facades, no horizon, no isometric tilt. BOTTOM LEFT DIAGONAL: expansive 45-degree isometric hand-drawn city scene with many blocks, bridge and high-rise neighborhood, actual building volumes visible. BOTTOM RIGHT STREET: human eye-level architectural illustration in square, several expressive outlined people, interesting workshop and library entrances, shaded seating, pedestrians and distant towers. Ground-level life warm and lively with illustrated architectural details. Make perspectives unmistakably different while all share the same style and landmarks. Thin cream gutters and understated legible typography outside scenes. Footer 'Ink city — concept art'. No brand logos, no UI, no heavy blur, no excessive text, no generic medieval fantasy. Distinctive professional art-direction exploration for a playable large city.
