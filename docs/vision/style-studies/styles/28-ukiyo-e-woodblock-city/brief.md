# Ukiyo-e-inspired woodblock city — brief

Original exploration intent; historical architectural details may be superseded
by shared comparison rules and the dated reviews. This migration does not select
a production style.

A riverfront city drawn as a woodblock print. Rice-paper cream, indigo, Prussian-blue water, vermilion banners, muted green, and pink blossom form the pigment set. Carved contour lines, flat perspective planes, patterned wave and cloud bands, and visible wood grain define the surface.

Buildings are timber halls with tiled roofs, stacked towers, a stone arched bridge, and workshop chimneys with rolled smoke. Interiors show tatami floors, low tables, and sliding screens. People wear patterned robes with wave motifs and carry parasols; the agent is a white-faced robot in a blue printed robe. Kana banners label workshops and squares.

Large views should hold flat planes and stylized water so map and diagonal read as one print. Street and night scenes should keep lantern light as flat red and orange shapes. Close-ups, including kiosks and phones, should stay linework with paper texture rather than shaded rendering.

**Proposed device adaptation:** preserve contour lines, flat pigment planes, and patterned water at every tier. Lower tiers would drop paper grain and secondary pattern; higher tiers could add registration offset and ink bleed. Specialist review and collaboration with artists from the tradition are a precondition for any production use. The generated sheets lean on borrowed motifs, including Mount Fuji, cherry blossom, and period dress, so the city reads as historical Edo rather than an original riverfront, and the vertical AR tabletop labels come out garbled.
